FAQ

Common Questions

What document types are supported?
Currently SatyaDoc supports Caste Certificates, Income Proof documents, and Degree/Educational Certificates. More document types including Domicile, Ration Card, and Disability certificates are coming soon.
Is my document stored on your servers?
No. Documents are processed in real-time and are never stored on any server. Analysis happens transiently and all data is discarded immediately after results are returned.
What does the 20% mismatch threshold mean?
For biometric parameters like face detection and signature matching, if the AI finds more than 20% deviation from what's expected (based on document type and issuing authority templates), it flags the parameter as suspicious or failed. This threshold is calibrated to minimize false positives.
Can I use this as legal proof of forgery?
SatyaDoc is an AI-powered screening tool, not a legal forensic instrument. A "Forged" verdict should be used to initiate a formal investigation by competent authorities — not as standalone legal evidence.
How accurate is the detection?
Accuracy depends on document quality and type. The system is most accurate for high-resolution scans of common document types. It's designed to flag anomalies for human review rather than make autonomous decisions.
Who is this tool for?
Government officials processing welfare scheme applications, HR teams verifying candidate credentials, NGOs doing field verification, and citizens who want to verify documents they receive.
